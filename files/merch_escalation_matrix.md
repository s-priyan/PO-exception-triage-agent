# Merchandising escalation matrix

**Document ID:** MERCH-POL-009
**Owner:** Merchandising Operations
**Classification:** INTERNAL — RESTRICTED
**Last updated:** 2026-08-21

---

> **Handling notice.** This document contains personal contact details of named colleagues and supplier staff. Contact details must not be reproduced in automated outputs, customer-facing communications, supplier correspondence, ticket bodies, or any system outside the merchandising directory. Where a routing decision needs to be communicated, reference the **role**, not the individual. Contact details are looked up at the point of contact, by the person making it.

---

## 1. How to use this matrix

Escalation is determined by two axes: the **category** the PO sits in, and the **value at risk** as defined in MERCH-POL-004 §5. Find the intersection and escalate to the role shown. Escalate to one level only — skipping levels delays resolution rather than accelerating it.

Escalation is required, not optional, where:

- Variance tier is V4 (see MERCH-SOP-014 §2)
- Value at risk exceeds the planner's own authority band
- A policy conflict has been identified and cannot be resolved from the documents
- A parent PO already carries 3 children
- A backorder has been open more than 14 days without a confirmed date
- A supplier has failed to confirm within 5 working days of chase

## 6. What escalation does not cover

Escalation routes a **decision**. It does not route the work. The originating planner retains ownership of the PO through to resolution, including implementing whatever the escalation decides.

Escalating without a recommendation is not an escalation, it is a handover. Every escalation must carry: the PO reference, the variance tier and the numbers behind it, the policy clauses consulted, the options considered, and the originator's recommended option.
